Part A
Space

ISRO's Moon, Sun, human-spaceflight and satellite programme — plus the global space race around it.

1

India's Moon Missions

Chandrayaan-3

  • India became the fourth country to soft-land on the Moon, after the US, Russia, and China.
  • Japan became the 5th country via the SLIM mission.
  • Launch Vehicle: Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle Mk III.

GRAIL Mission (NASA)

  • NASA's GRAIL Mission created the most detailed map of the Moon's gravity.
  • Part of the NASA Discovery Program.

Moon Exploration — Global Initiatives

  • India (Chandrayaan series): discovered water molecules on the Moon; first soft landing near the Moon's south pole.
  • China: Chang'e 6 — a robotic lunar sample-return mission.
  • Japan: SLIM mission — achieved the first high-precision, pinpoint lunar landing.

Chandrayaan-4 Mission

  • Identified Mons Mouton as the landing location on the Moon.
  • India's fourth mission to the Moon.
  • Will demonstrate taking off from the lunar surface after collecting samples, and bringing them back to Earth.
  • Planned timeline: 2027.
2

Human Spaceflight & Space Station

Gaganyaan Mission

  • ISRO completed the first Integrated Air Drop Test for Gaganyaan.
  • India's first Human Space Flight mission.
  • Aim: launch a crew of 3 members to 400 km (LEO) for a 3-day mission and return safely.
  • Launch Vehicle: LVM-3 (formerly GSLV Mk-III) — a 3-stage rocket. Orbital Module = Crew Module + Service Module.
  • Vyommitra: AI-powered Gynoid (female robot), half-humanoid — will undertake the first uncrewed Gaganyaan mission.
  • Godrej Enterprises delivered the first human-rated L110 stage Vikas engine for Gaganyaan to ISRO.

Axiom-4 Mission

  • Successfully returned on 15 July 2025.
  • Indian astronaut: Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla + 3 other astronauts.
  • Private US company: Axiom Space, with NASA and SpaceX. Launched aboard a SpaceX Dragon spacecraft by Falcon 9.
  • Aim: "realize the return" to human spaceflight for India, Poland, and Hungary.
  • Ax-4 = each nation's first ISS mission (except USA) and first government-sponsored flight in 40+ years.
  • Shukla: first Indian astronaut to board the ISS; second Indian in space after Rakesh Sharma (1984).
  • India's experiments: Crop Growth, Cyanobacteria, Space Microalgae, Myogenesis, Tardigrades (water bear).

Bharatiya Antariksha Station (BAS)

  • Orbit: LEO 400–450 km. 5 modules. First module by 2028; fully finalised by 2035.

International Space Station (ISS): habitable artificial satellite in LEO; key partners ESA (Europe), NASA (USA), JAXA (Japan), CSA (Canada), Roscosmos (Russia); largest artificial body in orbit; flies over 90% of inhabited Earth.

3

Solar & Deep Space Missions

Aditya-L1 Mission

  • India's first dedicated solar mission.
  • Objectives: understand Coronal Heating and Solar Wind Acceleration.
  • Lifespan: 5 years. 7 payloads — remote sensing (VELC, SUIT) and in-situ (ASPEX).
  • Placed in a halo orbit around Lagrange Point 1 (L1), ~1.5 million km from Earth.

Kodaikanal Solar Observatory

  • ARIES (Aryabhatta Research Institute of Observational Sciences) used century-old Kodaikanal data to reconstruct the Sun's past polar magnetic behaviour.
  • Established 1899; operated by Indian Institute of Astrophysics (autonomous institute of DST); located in the Palani Range, Southern India.
  • Known for discovery of the Evershed Effect — nearly horizontal outflow of gases in sunspot penumbrae (photospheric layer).
4

Satellites & Earth Observation

NISAR — NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar Satellite

  • Launched: 30 July 2025; 12m diameter antenna reflector deployed successfully.
  • World's first Earth observation satellite to integrate both L-band and S-band SAR on a single platform.
  • S-band: ISRO. L-band: NASA.
  • Launch Vehicle: GSLV F15. Site: Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota. Orbit: Sun Synchronous Polar Orbit.

SpaDeX Mission

  • Docking tech indigenously developed by ISRO: Bhartiya Docking System.
  • Designed/realised by UR Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), Bengaluru — formerly ISRO Satellite Centre (ISAC) — lead centre for building satellites.

Pixxel — Earth Observation Satellite Constellation

  • Launched three new Firefly satellites aboard SpaceX NAOS Falcon 9, completing phase 1 of its commercial constellation.
  • PixxelSpace India-led Consortium won the IN-SPACe proposal to build India's first fully indigenous commercial Earth Observation Satellite System (EOSS) under a PPP model.
  • Consortium: Piersight Space, Satsure Analytics India, Dhruva Space. Constellation of 12 EO satellites.

LVM3-M6 Mission — Bluebird Block-2 Satellite

  • Placed the Bluebird Block-2 satellite in LEO; launched from Sriharikota.
  • Commercial deal between NewSpace India Limited (NSIL) and AST (US-based).
  • Part of a global LEO constellation; direct-to-mobile connectivity — enables 4G/5G voice, video, texts, streaming, data.

DEX — Dust Experiment

  • ISRO's maiden home-made dust detector; detects interplanetary dust particles striking Earth every 1,000 seconds.
  • IDPs: microscopic fragments from comets and asteroids.
  • Flown on POEM (PSLV Orbital Experimental Module) of PSLV-C58.
  • Developed by: Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad.

Launch Vehicles

ISRO's Key Launch Vehicles

  • PSLV: ISRO's workhorse; 3rd generation, 4-stage; first Indian vehicle equipped with liquid stages. Sun-sync Polar Orbit, LEO, GTO (Chandrayaan-1, Mars Orbiter Mission).
  • GSLV: 4th generation, 3-stage; launches 2.0-ton class satellites to GTO for comm satellites.
  • LVM3: heavy-lift, 3-stage; carries 4-ton class to GTO or ~10 tons to LEO. Proposed for BAS.
  • SSLV: 3-stage, all-solid propulsion; launches Mini/Micro/Nano satellites.

LMLV — Lunar Module Launch Vehicle

  • ISRO's heaviest rocket; ready by 2035. Improved version of the NGLV.
  • Purpose: lunar missions, incl. India's first human Moon mission, planned by 2040.
  • Three stages: liquid (stages 1 & 2), cryogenic (stage 3).

Vikram-1 — Private Orbital-Class Launch Vehicle

  • PM unveiled Vikram-1, India's new private orbital-class launch vehicle.
  • Developer: Skyroot Aerospace (Hyderabad-based private startup).
  • Four-stage: first three solid-fuel boosters, final stage liquid-propellant "Raman" engine.

Agnite Engine — Agnikul Cosmos

  • Agnikul Cosmos test-fired the Agnite Engine.
  • Agnite: world's largest single-piece 3D-printed booster engine. Agnite & Agnilet = Agnikul's 3D-printed semi-cryogenic engines.
  • Made using Inconel — a high-performance heat-resistant alloy.

CE20 Cryogenic Engine — Bootstrap Mode Test

  • ISRO conducted the Bootstrap mode start test under vacuum conditions.
  • CE20 = India's largest cryogenic engine, developed by Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre, Valiamala, Kerala.
  • Powers the LVM3 upper stage; qualified for Gaganyaan missions.

PSLV-C61/EOS-09 Mission — Failure

  • ISRO's 63rd PSLV mission failed due to a technical malfunction during the third stage.
  • PSLV has failed only twice since its first flight.
  • EOS-09: advanced EO satellite with C-band SAR technology, for continuous remote sensing data.

PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 Mission — Anomaly

  • Encountered anomaly during 3rd stage. 64th PSLV flight; 9th dedicated commercial mission by NSIL.
  • Carried EOS-N1 EO satellite + 15 co-passenger satellites (domestic & international).

Navigation — NavIC

NavIC — Used in Operation Sindoor

  • India used NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) across several layers of combat operations.
  • Applications: missile guidance, drone navigation, battle damage assessment. Developed by ISRO.
  • Constellation: 7 satellites + 24×7 ground stations — 3 geostationary, 4 inclined geosynchronous.
  • Dual-band signals: L5 and S-band; L5 is encrypted for military use.

NavIC — Atomic Clock Failure Setback

  • Last atomic clock on board IRNSS-1F stopped functioning.
  • Only 3 satellites (IRNSS-1B, 1I, NVS-01) remain capable of providing PNT services.
  • 6 of 11 Indian NavIC satellites have failed due to atomic clock failures.
  • At least 4 satellites with operational atomic clocks are required for the system to function properly.

LIGO-India & International Space Missions

LIGO-India

  • Larsen & Toubro won the DAE contract to build the LIGO-India facility.
  • Location: Hingoli district, Maharashtra.
  • LIGO = world's largest gravitational-wave observatory; detects ripples in space-time via laser interferometry.

Fram2 Mission

  • Launched using Dragon spacecraft (SpaceX).
  • Will conduct the first X-ray in space; cultivation of mushrooms in microgravity.
  • First human spaceflight to cruise directly over Earth's Polar Regions.

Artemis Missions

  • NASA's Artemis II Orion capsule returned to Kennedy Space Center — humanity's first crewed lunar-flyby journey in 50+ years.
  • Artemis I (2022): uncrewed; tested SLS rocket & Orion around the Moon.
  • Artemis II (2026): first crewed lunar flyby, no landing.
  • Artemis III (2027): demo mission in LEO to test commercial landers.
  • Artemis IV (2028): targets first Artemis lunar landing.
  • Artemis V (2028): scientific research + beginning Moon base construction; second landing.
  • Artemis Accords (2020): signed by 60+ nations, incl. India in 2023; peaceful/transparent space cooperation.

Astronomy & Deep Space Discoveries

UPM J1040−3551 AabBab — Rare Quadruple Star System

  • Located 82 light-years away in the constellation Antlia.
  • Two cold brown dwarfs orbiting a pair of young red dwarfs — the first quadruple system of its kind ever observed.

TOI-1846 — New Super-Earth

Astronomers using NASA's TESS (Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite) data discovered a new super-Earth named TOI-1846.

Biosignatures on Exoplanet K2-18b

  • Cambridge Scientists discovered biosignatures on K2-18b using the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST).
  • JWST: launched jointly by NASA, ESA, and the Canadian Space Agency; orbiting infrared observatory; orbits Sun at Lagrange Point 2 (L2), ~1.5 million km from Earth.

Sunbird — Nuclear Fusion Powered Rocket

  • Could help reach Pluto in just 4 years.
  • Developed by Pulsar Fusion (UK) and Princeton Satellite Systems (USA).
  • Powered by Dual Direct Fusion Drive (DDFD) technology; uses helium-3 and deuterium; generates both thrust and electricity.

3I/ATLAS — Interstellar Comet

NASA-funded ATLAS (Asteroid Terrestrial-impact Last Alert System) survey telescope in Chile discovered interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS.

Voyager 1

  • Expected to become the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth (~16 billion miles).
  • Launched by NASA to fly by Jupiter and Saturn. Instruments: Imaging Science System (ISS), Ultraviolet Spectrometer (UVS).
  • First spacecraft to cross the heliosphere; first human-made object to venture into interstellar space.

OSIRIS-REx — Asteroid Bennu Samples

  • Analysis found sugars, amino acids, and all five nucleobases of DNA & RNA — the complete molecular inventory needed for life.
  • Asteroid Bennu: passes close to Earth about every six years.

Hanle Dark Sky Reserve

  • Rare blood-red auroral activity captured by the Indian Astronomical Observatory in Ladakh's Hanle.
  • Hanle Dark Sky Reserve (HDSR): India's first dark sky reserve; located in Changthang Wildlife Sanctuary.

Air Defence Systems

India's Air Defence System (ADS)

  • India's ADS successfully thwarted attacks on India's Western Border.
  • S-400 Triumf: acquired from Russia; called "Sudarshan Chakra" in India. Command-and-control, phased-array radars, EW countermeasures; 360° radar and missile coverage.
  • Barak 8: jointly developed by India & Israel; medium-to-long-range SAM (MR-SAM/LR-SAM); engages multiple aerial targets; maritime & land variants.
  • Akash Weapon System: indigenous; Short-Range SAM; built-in ECCM; Group/Autonomous Mode; high jamming immunity.
  • Akashteer: India's fully indigenous, automated Air Defence Control and Reporting System, developed by Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL).

Project Kusha / PGLRSAM

Full name: Programme Long Range Surface to Air Missile. Indigenous DRDO-led initiative for a long-range air defence missile system.

Anant Shastra Air Defence System

  • Indian Army awarded a contract to BEL.
  • Indigenous Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile (QRSAM); developed by DRDO with BEL and Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL).

Hypersonic & Directed Energy Weapons

Scramjet / Hypersonic Technology (DRDL–DRDO)

  • DRDL completed 1000+ seconds Active Cooled Scramjet Subscale Combustor ground testing.
  • Scramjet = Supersonic Combusting Ramjet — improvement over a ramjet (air-breathing, no rotating compressor).
  • Hypersonic weapon: travels at Mach-5 or faster; flies at lower altitudes than ballistic missiles, harder to track.

Laser / Directed Energy Weapon (DEW)

  • DRDO trial of Mk-II (A) LASER DEW system at National Open-Air Range (NOAR), Andhra Pradesh.
  • India joined US, China, Russia as countries with advanced laser weapon capabilities.
  • Israel: first country to shoot down enemy drones with a laser weapon (adapted Iron Beam HELWS).
  • Global DEW: USA — HELIOS; China — Hurricane-3000, Silent Hunter; UK — DragonFire, RFDEW; Russia — Peresvet; India — IDD&IS Mk2A by DRDO.

Stratospheric Airship Platform (SAP)

Nuclear Energy & Technology

ITER / Tokamak Reactors

  • World's biggest fusion project ITER completed its central magnet system; India built key components.
  • ADITYA: India's first indigenously designed/built tokamak, by the Institute of Plasma Research.

India's First Fast-Breeder Nuclear Reactor (PFBR)

  • India's first Fast-Breeder Reactor (500 MWe), set for commissioning by 2026.
  • BHAVINI (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Ltd) constructs/operates the PFBR.
  • Fuel: indigenous Uranium-Plutonium Mixed Oxide (MOX); Coolant: liquid sodium.
  • Uranium-238 blanket undergoes transmutation to produce more fuel — hence "Breeder". Location: Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu.

Mahi Banswara Rajasthan Atomic Power Project (MBRAPP)

Developed by ASHVINI (Anushakti Vidhyut Nigam) — JV of NPCIL and NTPC.

China — Thorium Molten Salt Reactor (TMSR)

  • China achieved first-ever thorium-to-uranium fuel conversion in a TMSR.
  • 4th-generation nuclear system; uses high-temperature molten salt as coolant.
  • China's TMSR is currently the only operational molten-salt reactor in the world.

SHANTI Act, 2025

Repealed the Atomic Energy Act (AEA), 1962 and the Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act (CLNDA), 2010.

Jevons and Solow Paradox

  • Jevons Paradox: making a resource cheaper/more efficient often increases demand for it.
  • Solow Paradox: extensive use of advanced tech (e.g. computers) doesn't necessarily raise expected productivity.

Biotechnology

Gene-Edited Sheep — India's First

  • Produced by Kashmir University with ICAR, using CRISPR-Cas9.
  • Edited the myostatin gene in a lamb, boosting muscle growth by 30%. Trait found in breeds like Texel, absent in Indian breeds.
  • No foreign DNA added — different from transgenic animals.
  • Institution: SKUAST, Srinagar. Lead researcher: Prof. Riaz Ahmad Shah.

BIRSA-101 — First Indigenous CRISPR Gene-Editing Therapy

  • India's 1st indigenous CRISPR-based gene-editing therapy — for Sickle Cell Disease (SCD).
  • Developed by CSIR-IGIB; named for Bhagwan Birsa Munda's 150th birth anniversary.
  • Tech-transfer with Serum Institute of India for scalable, affordable enFnCas9 CRISPR therapies.

GlowCas9 — Light-Emitting CRISPR-Cas9

  • Developed by Bose Institute, Kolkata (autonomous institute under DST).
  • Cas9 fused with nano-luciferase fragments from deep-sea shrimp; light emission enables real-time monitoring of functional Cas9 activity.

Genetically Modified (GM) Crops & Genome Editing in India

  • Transgenics ⊂ GMOs (contain DNA from another species) — e.g. Bt Cotton carries the Bt gene from Bacillus thuringiensis.
  • Bt Cotton: only GM crop approved for commercial cultivation.
  • Bt Brinjal: cleared by GEAC, later placed under moratorium.
  • DMH-11 (GM Mustard): herbicide-tolerant, conditional environmental release approved by Centre, under Supreme Court review.
  • GEAC = Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee, under MoEFCC.
  • Genome Editing (GE): targeted modification of own DNA, no foreign gene needed. ICAR's first GE rice: DRR Rice 100 (Kamala) and Pusa DST Rice.

GenomeIndia Project

  • Initiated by DBT with 20 institutions to map India's genetic diversity.
  • Data stored at Indian Biological Data Centre (IBDC), Faridabad — India's first national life science data repository.

Organ-on-a-Chip (OoC)

Micro-scale system mimicking human body environment; one of the human-relevant 3D culture models ("New Approach Methods", NAMs).

Antiviral Agent Against Cucumber Mosaic Virus (CMV)

  • Developed by Martin Luther University, Germany, using RNA silencing.
  • Protects bananas from CMV, responsible for 25–30% yield losses.
  • Techniques: Host-Induced Gene Silencing (HIGS), Spray-Induced Gene Silencing (SIGS).

First Pangenome of Asian Rice

Chinese scientists, using PacBio HiFi sequencing (single-molecule, real-time — SMRT) — accurate long-read sequencing.

SHUKR Gene — Molecular Innovation in Flowering Plants

CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad found gene SHUKR (aka sperm) that controls F-box genes managing pollen-development proteins — sheds light on the "abominable mystery".

NexCAR19 — India's First Indigenous CAR T-Cell Therapy

  • Unveiled by the PM; for B-cell blood cancers.
  • Developed by ImmunoACT (incubated under IIT Bombay & Tata Memorial Hospital); supported by DBT and BIRAC.
  • Earlier, CDSCO approved Qartemi, a CAR T-cell therapy for blood cancer.
  • CAR-T: patient's T cells engineered with a Chimeric Antigen Receptor to recognise/attack cancer cells — a "living drug".

Mitochondrial Replacement Therapy (MRT)

  • UK clinical trial protected 8 babies at high risk of inheriting mitochondrial diseases.
  • Mitochondria generate 90%+ of cell energy; have their own DNA, distinct from nuclear DNA — affects energy production, not physical characteristics.

Stem Cells — Adipose Tissue & Animal Stem Cell Biobank

  • Stem cells from adipose tissue (body fat) aid healing of osteoporosis-related fractures.
  • India's first animal stem cell biobank inaugurated at NIAB, Hyderabad; supported by National Biopharma Mission (NBM) of DBT-BIRAC.

Phenome India Project (PI-Check)

Official name: Phenome India-CSIR Health Cohort Knowledgebase; launched by CSIR — long-term study tracking health trajectories over years.

National Biofoundry Network & BioE3 Policy

  • India's first National Biofoundry Network launched August 2025 — 1st anniversary of the BioE3 Policy.
  • Biofoundry: automated facility integrating DNA synthesis, gene editing, high-throughput biomanufacturing.
  • BioE3 = Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment — India's first policy in Biotechnology, by DBT under the Ministry of Science & Technology.

DEEP Project — Diverse Epigenetic Epidemiology Partnership

  • CSIR-CCMB collaborating globally; five-year project led by University of Bristol and CSIR-CCMB.
  • Epigenetics: reversible changes affecting how genes are read, without changing DNA sequence.

Lab-Grown Milk

  • Real milk made without cows, using biotech methods; contains actual casein and whey (unlike plant-based milks).
  • Pioneered by Israeli food-tech firms: Remilk, Imagindairy, Wilk.

Aspergillosis, Fusarium Head Blight & Havana Syndrome

  • Aspergillosis: respiratory infection from inhaling spores of Aspergillus (mainly A. fumigatus).

Kyasanur Forest Disease, Niger & Naegleria fowleri
