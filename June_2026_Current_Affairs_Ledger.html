Major National Events

June 2026
June 1 – 5
  • Telangana's 12th Foundation Day was celebrated on June 2; the state was formed on June 2, 2014 under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act.
  • Kolkata is set to become the 18th city in India connected to a water metro network under the Centre's National Urban Water Transport Programme, operating across the Hooghly River.
  • The Odisha government approved restoration of English spellings of 64 locations to align with original Odia pronunciation, to preserve linguistic heritage.
  • Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched the 'Khet Bachao Abhiyan' (June 1–30) from Ramasiya village, Raisen district (MP), focusing on soil health, curbing chemical overuse, and natural farming awareness.
  • Centre gave final financial approval for the Chenab–Beas Link Tunnel (8.7 km) in Lahaul-Spiti, Himachal Pradesh, involving a investment of Rs. 2352 cr .
  • SC JudgmentIn Prajwala vs Union of India (29 May 2026), the Supreme Court approved a binding plan against human trafficking focused on survivor rehabilitation, not punishment — holding trafficking victims have a fundamental right to rehabilitation under Article 21 (life with dignity) and Article 23 (prohibition of trafficking).
  • MoJS signed MoUs with 5 states/UTs — Arunachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Tamil Nadu, Nagaland, Puducherry — to ensure water quality/supply under Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0.
  • The 8th India–Myanmar Bilateral Drug Control Cooperation meeting was held in New Delhi (2 June 2026); India led by Anurag Garg (DG, Narcotics Control Bureau), Myanmar by Thant Lwin Maung (Joint Secretary, CCDAC).
  • South Africa's Deputy President Paul Shipokosa Mashatile met President Droupadi Murmu at Rashtrapati Bhavan; discussed Global South development and cooperation in tech, pharma, energy and MSMEs.
  • MeitY's BHASHINI Division (Digital India Corporation), with industry partners, launched the VYOMA Innovation Challenge for open-source, multilingual, voice-first AI solutions that work offline / in low connectivity.
  • President Murmu inaugurated the National Conclave on Strengthening of ITDA & Integrated Tribal Development Projects at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi (3 June 2026).
    • Also inaugurated a training fab at the Centre for Nano Science and Engineering, IISc Bengaluru.
    • Inaugurated 75 space labs in Eklavya Model Residential Schools.
  • Laos Deputy PM & Foreign Minister Thongsavan Phomvihane met President Murmu in New Delhi.
  • First meeting of the BRICS Anti-Corruption Working Group held virtually, chaired by DoPT Secretary Radha S. Chauhan; theme — "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability."
  • Padma Shri Sudarsan Pattnaik created a sand sculpture themed 'BRICS India 2026' on Puri beach, Odisha to welcome BRICS delegates.
  • Union Cabinet approved an Aviation Fuel Price Stabilization Support Fund of ₹10,000 crore to help airlines against ATF price fluctuations.
  • Central approval granted under the Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) for a new 163.18 km coastal highway from Rameswar to Paradip, Odisha.
  • A high-level committee headed by former IAS officer S. Radha Chauhan was constituted to probe irregularities in CBSE's On-Screen Marking (OSM) tender process.
  • MDoNER, Ministry of Textiles & Assam govt launched 'Mission Senehjori' to brand Assam's golden Muga silk — the world's only natural golden silk — as a premium global luxury brand.
    • TrapAssam produces roughly 90% of the world's Muga silk.
June 6 – 10
  • India hosted the 13th BRICS Urbanisation Forum in New Delhi (11–12 June 2026) under its BRICS presidency; theme — "Cities for People: BRICS Cooperation for an Inclusive and Resilient Urban Future" (announced by Minister Manohar Lal).
  • Minister Pralhad Joshi announced a target to install rooftop solar in 75 lakh (7.5 million) households by December 2026 under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana; launched a new logo and WhatsApp chatbot 'SUNtosh' marking 2 years of the scheme.
  • FirstChandigarh University became India's first private university to launch the 'IndiaAI Data Lab' with Intel India.
  • FirstChumur village, Eastern Ladakh (near the India–China border), became India's first 'Model Border Village'.
  • Centre approved a 'Clean Mobility Scheme' worth ₹19,585 crore to phase out old commercial vehicles and cut pollution in Delhi-NCR.
  • Kerala CM V.D. Satheesan launched 'Operation Toofan: The Narco Hunt', an anti-drug initiative targeting the state's youth.
  • Nagaland imposed a complete one-year ban on manufacture, storage & sale of tobacco/nicotine food products, citing health risks.
  • Union Minister Manohar Lal Khattar launched the 'Swachh App' to resolve geo-tagged waste complaints within 2 hours in West Bengal municipalities (piloted in 10 municipal bodies).
  • PM unveiled development projects worth ₹2,970 crore in Daman, including the new NAMO Airport terminal (₹1,340 crore) and NAMO Hospital; laid foundation stone for ₹885 crore port projects on Kalpeni and Kadmat islands, Lakshadweep.
  • Oil India Limited confirmed discovery of significant natural gas reserves in the 'Sri Vijayapuram-3' block, 15 km off the east coast of the Andaman Islands.
  • Home Minister Amit Shah presided over the 73rd plenary session of the North Eastern Council in Shillong; deliberated on the 'North East Vision Plan-2047'.
  • Skill Development Minister Jayant Chaudhary launched 'Navachar Mantra' to support grassroots innovators in Tier-2/3 cities & rural areas — implemented by NIESBUD with FITT, IIT Delhi as technical partner.
  • Under 'Ease of Doing Business', govt abolished 'Inspector Raj', converting inspectors into 'Inspector-cum-Facilitators' to assist rather than penalise businesses.
  • CSIR-CRRI and the Haryana govt signed an MoU — "Implementation of Standard Framework for Paving and Greening of Urban Roads (Haryana State)" — to combat road dust pollution in the NCR.
  • Under Ayushman Bharat, over 44.14 crore Ayushman cards generated; over 60 crore non-communicable disease screenings conducted at Ayushman Arogya Mandirs.
  • Lowest TFRPer NFHS-6, the Andaman & Nicobar Islands recorded the lowest Total Fertility Rate (TFR) of 0.9 — less than half the replacement level of 2.1.
  • With West Bengal's inclusion, Ayushman Bharat–PM Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) is now implemented across all states/UTs — WB became the 36th and final state/UT to join.
    • AB-PMJAY provides free cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh/family/year at empanelled govt & private hospitals for serious illnesses (e.g. cancer, kidney transplants).
  • Amit Shah launched the 'Land Port Management System', named 'Vinimay', to streamline cross-border trade, passenger movement, and cargo processing.
  • First major meeting of the 'Policy Implementation and Monitoring Committee' under the National Cooperative Policy-2025 held in New Delhi — vision: 'Sahakar se Samriddhi'.
  • A technical breakthrough was achieved in constructing the 13 km strategic Zojila Tunnel, connecting Baltal (Kashmir) and Minamarg (Ladakh) for all-weather connectivity.
June 11 – 15
  • RecordOn 10 June 2026, PM Narendra Modi became India's longest-serving elected PM, completing 4,399 days continuous tenure — surpassing Jawaharlal Nehru's record of 4,398 days.
  • Centre notified a 'Resettlement and Rehabilitation Census' in the strategically important Nicobar Islands related to an infrastructure project.
  • The 11th Governing Council meeting of NITI Aayog concluded in New Delhi (11 June 2026), chaired by PM Modi; aim — cooperative federalism for 'Viksit Bharat @2047'; theme — "Inclusive Human Development for Viksit Bharat @2047."
  • Guinness RecordRapido, with the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, launched a nationwide awareness drive for the 'Rahveer Scheme' (road accident victim assistance) — setting a Guinness World Record as 4 lakh "Captains" took the pledge.

  • India outlined a strategic framework under its updated Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC) 3.0 targets to expand non-fossil fuel energy capacity by 2030:
    • Achieve 60% of cumulative installed electric power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2035.
    • Reduce GDP emission intensity by 47% from 2005 levels by 2035.
    • Create an additional carbon sink of 3.5–4.0 billion tonnes CO₂-eq through forest/tree cover by 2035.
  • At UNEP's Intergovernmental Negotiating Committee (INC) meeting, India announced tightening its Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) framework to eliminate single-use plastics — companies must directly meet recycling targets; ban on End-of-Life (EoL) disposal certificates.
  • An all-women 'Singapen' special task force was launched in Tamil Nadu for women's safety — staffed exclusively by female police personnel, under direct CM supervision.
  • Centre is developing mega leather, footwear & accessories clusters in Bihar (Muzaffarpur), Madhya Pradesh (Sitapur, Morena), Maharashtra (Raigad) and Tamil Nadu (Ranipet) at a cost of ₹898 crore.
  • Record 6th titleIndia won the SAFF Women's Championship 2026, defeating defending champions Bangladesh 3–1 in the final at Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium (Margao), Goa — a record sixth title for India.
  • Telangana govt launched 'Stree Ride' (via the Telangana Women Safety Wing) for women's safety and economic self-reliance.
  • FirstVaranasi became the first city included in India's "Easy Connect Model" for international travel.
  • 33rdWest Bengal became the 33rd legislative assembly to adopt NeVA (National e-Vidhan Application).
  • Centre signed a tripartite MoU with Assam and Nagaland governments for exploration/production of crude oil and natural gas.

Defence Deals, Dialogues & Exercises
  • The 10th India-Thailand Defence Dialogue was held in Bangkok; India represented by Satyajit Mohanty, Thailand by Nuttapol Diewvanich.
  • Gujarat Police detained 362 illegal Bangladeshi nationals under 'Operation Delta Hunt'.
