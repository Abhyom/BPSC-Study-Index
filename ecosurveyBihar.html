⚠ MCQ Trap

Two GSDP figures exist for the same year: current prices (₹9,91,997 Cr, 13.1% growth) vs constant 2011–12 prices (₹5,31,372 Cr, 8.6% growth). Constant-price growth is always lower because it strips out inflation — examiners love swapping these numbers.

The Primary sector gives the most employment (54.2%) but contributes the least to GSVA (18.3%) — the classic low-productivity-agriculture trap. Tertiary is the opposite: highest GSVA share (54.8%) with modest employment share (22.2%).

💼 Entrepreneurship Schemes

Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana (MMUY)

Provides a loan amount of ₹10 lakh per beneficiary.

Sub-schemes: SC/ST, EBC, Yuva, Mahila (including transgender), and Minority Udyami.

Bihar Laghu Udyami Yojana (BLUY)

Targets small/micro entrepreneurs with a grant of ₹2 lakh per beneficiary (100% grant, no repayment). Disbursed in 3 instalments (₹50k + ₹1L + ₹50k) via DBT.

Chapter V

Labour, Employment & Skill Development

Folio 005

👩 Women Empowerment Schemes

Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana

Objective: Women economic empowerment (Linked with JEEViKA SHGs). Target is 1 woman per family.

  • Phase-1: ₹10,000 support per beneficiary. Covers 1.56 crore women. Total outlay: ₹15,600 crore.
  • Phase-2 (Proposed): Up to ₹2 lakh for enterprise expansion.
  • Eligibility: 18-60 years, non-taxpayer, not in government service.

📚 Skill Development

  • Jananayak Karpuri Thakur Skill University: Focuses on training in Energy 4.0 and Industry 4.0 sectors to handle emerging sunrise sectors.
  • Mukhyamantri Pratigya Yojana: Focuses on providing internships for the youth.

Longest NH in Bihar: NH 27EW (East-West Corridor) routing from Porbandar (Gujarat) to Silchar (Assam). Length in Bihar is 483 km.

Rural Roads: Almost 90% paved. The best performing district is Sheikhpura.

🚢 Waterways & Urban Transport

Jal Marg Vikas Project (JMVP)

Supported by the World Bank. Includes National Waterway-1 (NW-1): Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hooghly system.

Other National Waterways noted: NW-2 (Brahmaputra), NW-3 (West Coast Canal), NW-4 (Godavari-Krishna).

PM e-Bus Sewa

Objective: Decarbonising urban transport.

  • Total e-buses: 400
  • Coverage across 6 cities: Patna, Bhagalpur, Darbhanga, Gaya, Muzaffarpur, Purnea.

💻 Key E-Governance Initiatives

Emergency and Safety Systems

  • ERSS – Dial 112: Emergency Response Support System.
  • Cyber Prahar: 1930 Helpline and 44 cyber thanas.
  • FASTER System: Instant e-authenticated judicial orders (bail etc.) from Court to Jail.

Agricultural and Disaster Management Apps

  • BeFIQR App (2023): Real-time flood/irrigation alerts.
  • SACHET Platform: Location-specific alerts for lightning and rainfall.

Governance and Citizen Services

  • Bhu-Samadhan Portal: Land dispute resolution.
  • e-Nibandhan: Fully operational in all 141 offices; allows registering property from home.
  • AI Nibhrit: AI-driven masking of Aadhaar/PAN.
  • Right to Service Act: Bihar was the 2nd state in India (after MP) to enact the RTS Act. Service delivery timeline is 7-15 days.

Welfare, Health & Environment

  • Mission Vihaan: Monitors welfare, medical, and probation activities.
  • Dhawa Dal App: Rescue child labourers.
  • BHAVYA: Bihar Health Application Visionary Yojana for All; integrates 13,900+ health facilities.
  • e-Parivesh Portal: Single-window hub for environmental/forest clearances.

💡 Consumer Services & Schemes

  • 1912: Toll-free Helpline.
  • Awareness Campaign: "Voltage Bhaiya – Bijli Didi"
Mukhyamantri Vidyut Upbhokta Sahayata Yojana (MVUSY)
  • Free electricity up to 125 units/month for all domestic consumers (urban + rural).
  • Effective from July 2025.
  • ~90% of 1.89 Cr domestic consumers (~1.67 Cr households) get a zero bill.
  • Beyond 125 units: Subsidised rate shown as "State Government Grant".
  • Promotes 1.1 kW rooftop solar.

Major Solar Project

Kajra Solar Power Plant (Lakhisarai): Solar generation capacity of 495 MW. Features a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) of 301 MWh. Expected completion in January 2027.

Digital Access: 100% of Gram Panchayats (8,053 total) onboarded for digital access, beating the national average of 95.8%.

🌮 JEEViKA & Livelihoods

JEEViKA: Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society. Launched as a pilot in 2006, full implementation in 2011 under NRLM. It is an autonomous society aided by the World Bank.

Satat Jeevikoparjan Yojana (SJY)

Targets households dependent on country liquor/toddy and ultra-poor SC/ST families. Provides support of up to ₹2 lakh for self-employment.

🏘 Urbanization & Industrial Development

  • Bihar's urban percentage is 11.3%, making it the 2nd lowest nationally (after Himachal Pradesh).
  • Bag Cluster (Muzaffarpur): Located in Bela Industrial Area using a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model.

Public Distribution System (PDS)

Fortified Rice: Blended at a ratio of 1:100 (fortified to regular rice) to combat malnutrition. Enriched with Iron, Folic acid, and Vitamin B-12.
