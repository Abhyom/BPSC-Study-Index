National Family Health Survey (NFHS)

Folio 000 · BPSC Prelims

This ledger consolidates critical health, demographic, and socio-economic facts from the latest National Family Health Survey (NFHS) data into ten targeted chapters. Every headline number is highlighted in yellow, every positive trend or achievement in green, key metrics/terms in blue, and areas of concern, declines, or disparities in red — designed specifically for rapid MCQ recall.

📕 How This Ledger Is Organised

Use the sidebar to jump between the ten chapters (I–X), structured exactly according to the NFHS survey framework — covering population profiles, digital literacy, fertility, family planning, maternal and infant health, nutrition, NCDs, and women's empowerment.

The population in the under-15 age group has declined to 25.5% from the earlier 26.5%.

🏠 Basic Amenities & Inclusion

  • Electricity: 98.3% of the population lives in households with electricity (up from 96.8%).
  • Improved Drinking Water: Access has reached 96.5% (up from 95.9%).
  • Iodized Salt: 94.2% of households use it (slight drop from 94.3%).
Health Insurance & Financial Inclusion
  • Health Insurance coverage saw a massive increase to 60.2% (up from 41.0% in NFHS-5). Coverage is surprisingly higher in Rural areas (62.0%) compared to Urban areas (56.4%).
  • In 98.2% of households, at least one member has a bank or post office account.
  • In 18.8% of households, women own a house or land (solely or jointly).
Chapter II

Education and Digital Literacy

Folio 002

📚 Schooling (Age 6+)

73.7% of women have ever attended school. There is a stark divide: 84.3% in urban areas vs 69.2% in rural areas.

In the 15-49 age group, the percentage with 10 or more years of schooling is 46.4% for women, compared to 54.6% for men.

Chapter III

Marriage and Fertility

Folio 003

💍 Child Marriage Rates

Women (aged 20-24) married before age 18 declined to 20.1% (from 23.3%). However, the rural rate (23.3%) remains double that of urban areas (11.4%).

Among men (aged 25-29), child marriage dropped from 17.7% to 15.9%.

👶 Fertility Trends

Total Fertility Rate (TFR)
2.0
Stable (was 2.0 in NFHS-5)
⚠ MCQ Trap

TFR varies sharply by region: It is well below replacement in Urban areas (1.6) but remains at Rural areas (2.1).

Adolescent Motherhood: 6.7% of women aged 15-19 were either mothers or pregnant.

👪 Contraceptive Use (Aged 15-49)

Any method: Increased from 66.7% to 69.1%.

Chapter V

Maternal Health and Childbirth

Folio 005

🤸 Prenatal Care

  • Antenatal Care (ANC): First trimester check-ups rose from 70.0% to 76.2%. At least four ANC visits increased from 58.5% to 65.2%.
  • Iron-Folic Acid (IFA): Mothers consuming IFA for 180+ days increased from 26.0% to 37.8%.

🏥 Delivery Methods

Institutional Deliveries: Risen globally from 88.6% to 90.6%. However, deliveries in public health facilities declined from 61.9% to 58.6%.

Chapter VII

Child Nutrition (Malnutrition Standards)

Folio 007

🍆 Feeding Practices

  • Breastfeeding initiated within 1 hour: Rose from 41.8% to 50.1%.
  • Exclusive breastfeeding (up to 6 months): Declined from 63.7% to 55.8% (cause for concern).
  • Adequate Diet: Only 15.3% of children aged 6-23 months receive it (up slightly from 11.0%).

Chapter VIII

Adult Nutrition and NCDs

Folio 008

🤢 Nutritional Extremes

Undernutrition (BMI < 18.5)

Prevalence has risen to 19.7% among women (from 18.7%) and to 19.7% among men (from 16.2%).

Obesity (BMI ≥ 25.0)

Increasing rapidly. Risen to 30.7% among women (42.8% in urban areas) and 27.3% among men (from 22.9%).

🩺 Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)

  • Diabetes (Blood Sugar): High/very high levels found in 17.8% of women and 20.9% of men.
  • Hypertension (High BP): 19.4% for women & 22.1% for men.
Chapter IX

Women's Empowerment and Gender-Based Violence

Folio 009

👩‍💻 Agency and Inclusion

  • Household Decisions: 89.0% of married women participate in 3 key decisions.
  • Bank Accounts: Women using their own accounts rose from 78.6% to 89.0%.
  • Mobile Phones: Women using their own phones increased from 53.9% to 63.6%.
  • Menstrual Hygiene: 79.2% of women aged 15-24 use hygienic methods.

🚫 Domestic Violence

The percentage of women (aged 18-49) experiencing spousal violence has declined from 29.2% to 22.3%.
