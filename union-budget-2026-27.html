Section 02 · First Kartavya

Accelerate & Sustain Economic Growth

Six interventions — manufacturing in frontier sectors, capital goods, textiles, legacy clusters, SMEs, infrastructure, and energy security.

1 · Scaling up manufacturing in 7 strategic & frontier sectors

6 INTERVENTIONS
  • Biopharma SHAKTI (Strategy for Healthcare Advancement through Knowledge, Technology and Innovation) — outlay of ₹10,000 crore over 5 years to build India as a global biopharma manufacturing hub.
    • 3 new NIPER institutes + upgrade of 7 existing ones.
    • Network of 1000+ accredited India Clinical Trials sites.
  • India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0 — equipment, materials, full-stack Indian IP, supply-chain fortification, industry-led research & training centres.
  • Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme outlay increased to ₹40,000 crore.
  • Dedicated Rare Earth Corridors for Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu — mining, processing, research, manufacturing.
  • Scheme for 3 dedicated Chemical Parks via challenge route, cluster-based plug-and-play model.

  • Dedicated ₹1,000 crore SME Growth Fund to create future "Champion" enterprises.
  • Self-Reliant India Fund gets additional ₹2,000 crore for micro-enterprise risk capital.

Section 03 · Second Kartavya

Fulfil Aspirations, Build Capacity

Education-to-employment pipelines, healthcare capacity, and animal husbandry — the human-capital half of the budget.

Creation of Professionals for Viksit Bharat
High-Powered 'Education to Employment and Enterprise' Standing Committee — measures focused on the Services Sector as a core growth driver.
ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI to design short-term, modular courses & practical tools to build a cadre of 'Corporate Mitras', especially in Tier-II/III towns.
Existing Allied Health Professional (AHP) institutions upgraded, new ones set up in private & government sectors — 100,000 AHPs to be added.
5 Regional Medical Hubs to promote India as a medical-tourism destination.
3 new All India Institutes of Ayurveda to be established.

Animal husbandry

  • Availability of veterinary professionals to scale up by more than 20,000.
  • Loan-linked capital subsidy support scheme for veterinary/para-vet colleges, hospitals, diagnostic labs & breeding facilities in the private sector.
  • Training institutes set up as Regional Centres of Excellence for manpower development.

Banking & financial-sector reform

CROSS-LINK
High-level committee on banking for "Viksit Bharat" — comprehensive sector review aligned with India's next growth phase, while safeguarding financial stability, inclusion & consumer protection.
Restructuring of Power Finance Corporation and Rural Electrification Corporation to achieve scale and improve efficiency in public-sector NBFCs.
Comprehensive review of FEMA (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules — a more contemporary, user-friendly foreign-investment framework.
Municipal Bonds: incentive of ₹100 crore for a single bond issuance above ₹1,000 crore, to encourage higher-value municipal bonds from large cities.

Section 05 · Budget Speech Part B

Direct Taxes

New Income Tax Act rollout, TDS/TCS rationalisation, penalty & prosecution reform, cooperatives, and the IT-sector safe harbour package.

New Income Tax Act & ease of living

  • New Income Tax Act, 2025 comes into effect from April 2026; simplified rules & forms to be notified shortly for ordinary citizens.
  • Interest awarded by the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal to a natural person — exempt from Income Tax, TDS also removed.
  • Simplified TDS provisions for manpower supply — benefits labour-intensive businesses.
  • Rule-based automated process for Lower/Nil TDS deduction certificates for small taxpayers (replaces filing with assessor).
  • Single-window filing with depositories for Form 15G / 15H (TDS on dividends, interest, etc.).
  • Return-revision window extended from 31 December to 31 March, with a nominal fee.
  • Staggered timeline for filing of tax returns.
  • TAN for NRI property transactions replaced by resident-buyer PAN-based challan.
  • One-time 6-month foreign-asset disclosure scheme for small taxpayers.

TCS rationalization

⚠ MCQ Trap
Overseas tour packages: 20% → 2% is the steepest cut in the whole digest — a favourite for "which item saw the biggest rate reduction" questions.

Rationalizing penalty & prosecution

  • IT assessment & penalty proceedings integrated into one common order.
  • Taxpayers can update returns even after reassessment has started — at an additional 10% tax rate over the applicable rate.
  • Penalty for misreporting income also eligible for immunity, with payment of additional income tax.
  • Prosecution framework under the Income Tax Act rationalised.
  • Non-production of books/documents & TDS-in-kind payment requirement — decriminalised.
  • Non-disclosure of non-immovable foreign assets under ₹20 lakh — immunity from prosecution, retrospective from 1.10.2024.

Cooperatives

  • Deduction for primary cooperative societies supplying milk/oilseeds/fruits/vegetables extended to those supplying cattle feed and cotton seed.
  • Inter-cooperative society dividend income allowed as deduction under the new tax regime, to the extent further distributed to members.
  • 3-year dividend-income exemption for a notified national cooperative federation on investments made up to 31.1.2026.

Supporting the IT sector

SAFE HARBOUR
Software dev, ITeS, KPO & contract R&D for software clubbed as a single "Information Technology Services" category with a common safe-harbour margin of 15.5%.
Safe-harbour turnover threshold raised from ₹300 crore to ₹2,000 crore.
Safe harbour approval via automated, rule-driven process — valid for 5 years at a stretch.
Unilateral APA for IT services fast-tracked — target conclusion within 2 years (extendable by 6 months on request).

Attracting global business & investment

  • Foreign companies providing cloud services via India-based data centres get a safe harbour of 15% of cost (related-party case).
  • Safe harbour for non-residents on component warehousing in a bonded warehouse: 2% profit margin on invoice value → effective tax ≈ 0.7%, lower than competing jurisdictions.
  • 5-year income-tax exemption for non-residents supplying capital goods/equipment/tooling to toll manufacturers in a bonded zone.
  • Exemption on global income for a non-resident expert staying up to 5 years under notified schemes.
  • MAT exemption for all non-residents paying tax on presumptive basis.

Other tax proposals

  • Buyback taxed as Capital Gains for all shareholder types; promoters pay additional buyback tax — effective rate 22% (corporate) / 30% (non-corporate) promoters.
  • STT on futures raised to 0.05% (from 0.02%); STT on options premium raised to 0.15% (from 0.1%) and on exercise to 0.125%.
  • MAT proposed as a final tax — no further credit accumulation from 1 April 2026; final tax rate cut to 14% (from 15%). Brought-forward MAT credit up to 31 March 2026 stays usable, capped at 1/4th of tax liability in the new regime.
  • Joint Committee (MCA + CBDT) to align ICDS with Ind-AS; separate ICDS accounting scrapped from tax year 2027–28.
Section 06 · Budget Speech Part B

Indirect Taxes & Customs

Tariff simplification, a trust-based customs process, ease-of-doing-business digitisation, and new export opportunities.

Tariff simplification

  • Marine, leather & textile: duty-free import limit for seafood-export processing inputs raised from 1% to 3% of FOB value; duty-free footwear-export inputs scheme extended.
  • Energy transition: BCD exemption on capital goods for Lithium-Ion Cell manufacturing continued; BCD exempted on sodium antimonate (solar-glass manufacture).
  • Critical minerals: BCD exempted on capital goods for processing critical minerals.
  • Biogas-blended CNG: biogas value excluded entirely while computing Central Excise duty on biogas-blended CNG.
  • Civil & defence aviation: BCD exempted on components/parts for civilian, training & other aircraft, and on raw materials for aircraft-parts MRO by Defence-sector units.
  • BCD exempted on specified parts for microwave ovens.
  • SEZ: one-time measure allowing eligible SEZ manufacturing units to sell into the Domestic Tariff Area at concessional duty, within a prescribed quantity limit.
  • Ease of living: tariff on dutiable goods for personal use cut from 20% to 10%; BCD reduced on 17 drugs/medicines; duty-free personal import for 7 more rare diseases.

Customs process simplification — trust-based systems

  • Trusted-importer bill-of-entry filing auto-notifies Customs on goods arrival for compliance-free imports.
  • Warehousing framework shifts to operator-centric model — self-declarations, electronic tracking, risk-based audit.

Ease of doing business

  • Cargo clearance across government agencies via a single interconnected digital window, by end of FY.
  • Food, drugs, plant/animal/wildlife product clearance (~70% of interdicted cargo) on this system by April 2026.
  • Goods with no compliance requirement: cleared immediately after online registration.
  • Customs Integrated System (CIS) — single scalable platform, rollout over 2 years.
  • Non-intrusive scanning (advanced imaging + AI) expanded, aiming to scan every container at all major ports.

New export opportunities & ease of living

Fish catch by an Indian vessel in the EEZ or High Seas made duty-free; landing at a foreign port treated as an export of goods.
Value cap of ₹10 lakh per consignment on courier exports removed entirely — helps small businesses, artisans & startups sell via e-commerce.
Baggage-clearance rules for international travel revised — enhanced duty-free allowances for present-day travel realities.
Honest taxpayers can close disputes by paying an additional amount in lieu of penalty.
