India national data, side by side with Bihar — every indicator, colour-coded.

12 · Bihar — Key Analytical Notes (for reasoning-based MCQs)
Branding focus (BPSC Bhai style): Bihar's women's-status trajectory was earlier shaped by resource crunch, lower education, fewer jobs, weaker decision-making power and lower living standards — NFHS-6 shows a mixed recovery: strong gains in bank/mobile access and breastfeeding practices, but continued lags in ANC coverage, institutional delivery, and higher reported violence.
Breastfeeding advantage explained: Despite lower institutional-delivery rates, Bihar's early and exclusive breastfeeding figures beat the national average — attributed to ASHA-worker-driven POSHAN Abhiyan counselling and a lower female paid-employment rate (~16.5%) keeping more mothers at home in the first 6 months.
Financial inclusion vs asset ownership paradox: Near-universal bank accounts (Jan Dhan-driven) coexist with low land/house ownership by women (14.9%) — access to a financial instrument ≠ access to a real asset.
NCD paradox: Bihar's lower diabetes/hypertension prevalence versus the national average is linked to its rural, agrarian, physically active lifestyle rather than to better healthcare access.
